Get your card offers

Card Playbook can pull in every Amex, Chase and Capital One offer with one tap of a bookmark. It only reads the offers page you have open: it doesn't add offers, change your account, or send your info anywhere. Set it up once (about 2 minutes), then use it whenever you want fresh offers.

1Install the bookmark

On iPhone or iPad (Safari)

  1. Tap Copy bookmark code.
  2. Bookmark this page: tap Share (the square with an arrow) → Add Bookmark → Save.
  3. Tap the book icon → Edit (bottom right) → tap the bookmark you just saved.
  4. Change the name to Grab offers.
  5. Tap the address under the name, delete it all, and paste the code. Tap Done.

On a computer (Chrome, Safari, Edge)

Show your bookmarks bar, then drag this button onto it:

Grab offers

2Open your offers page

Use your bank's website in Safari or Chrome, not its app (bookmarks can't run inside bank apps). Sign in, then:

AmexMenu → Offers. Pick the card at the top if you have more than one. The bookmark skips the "Added to Card" row and reads the full list below it.

ChaseChoose the card, then open Chase Offers (under the card's rewards, or "See all offers"). The page shows something like "148 results."

Capital OneOpen Offers from your card (it opens capitaloneoffers.com).

Citi, Bank of America, U.S. Bank and Wells Fargo offer pages may work too.

3Tap the bookmark

  1. With the offers page open, tap Grab offers (iPhone: book icon → Grab offers).
  2. A small panel appears and the page scrolls by itself: "Reading offers… 57 of 148." Don't touch the page for 20 to 40 seconds.
  3. When it says "Found 148 Chase offers," tap Copy for Card Playbook.

4Paste into Card Playbook

  1. Open Card Playbook → Offers tab → Paste offers from your bank.
  2. Check the card it picked under "These are for," then tap Import offers.

This replaces that card's offer list, so expired offers drop off. Offers you marked "Used" stay that way. Do one card at a time: grab, paste, repeat for the next card.

?Tips and fixes

  • How often: about once a week per card, or before a big purchase.
  • "No offers found": make sure the offers list is on screen and you're signed in, then tap the bookmark again.
  • Paste does nothing: a box appears instead. Long-press it, tap Paste, then Read code.
  • Count looks wrong: tap "Something off? Copy troubleshooting details" on the panel and send it to whoever shared Card Playbook with you.
  • Stopped working after a while: banks redesign their sites. Come back here, copy the code again, and paste it over your bookmark's address to get the latest version.
Is this safe?

The bookmark runs only on the page you're looking at, only when you tap it, and only reads the offers shown there. It can't see your password, doesn't click anything on your account, and the offers list goes only to your clipboard. You paste it into Card Playbook yourself, and it stays on your phone.
